About

I am an AI/ML engineer and researcher with a background in Computer Engineering and experience in university teaching, AI/ML research and project-based engineering training.

I have worked with machine learning and deep learning models, explored large language model applications, and developed research around the stability of explanations in text classification. I enjoy taking a problem from its technical foundations through implementation and evaluation.

My particular interests are agentic AI, explainable AI, NLP and trustworthy machine learning. I am motivated by AI that is not only technically capable, but also understandable and useful in real contexts.
